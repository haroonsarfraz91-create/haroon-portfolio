About

I work between Chinese fintech operators and Pakistan’s SECP-regulated lending market. Over six years I moved from front-line verification to running operations for NBFC-licensed platforms, so I understand both how recovery numbers get made and what an auditor will ask about them.

Growth has been part of the job throughout. I have designed lead generation and telemarketing processes, written call scripts and promotional messages, and screened new applicants so that acquisition stayed inside the rules.

Today I oversee floor operations for a Chinese fintech’s Pakistan business, holding SOP adherence and regulatory posture steady while teams chase daily targets. Before that I built the QA function at a licensed digital lender: scorecards, sampling, calibration, a training programme and a corrective-action framework that took quality scores from 30% to 70% and cut violations by 40%.

I report in Urdu and English, to Pakistani management and to headquarters in China, and I build the MIS and KPI reporting that lets both see the floor clearly.
